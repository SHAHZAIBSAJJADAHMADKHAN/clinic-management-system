# Permanent Engineering Rules

1. PRD requirements take priority.
2. The official ten acceptance tests remain unchanged.
3. The backend enforces business rules and authorization.
4. Never expose secrets in source, logs, or client bundles.
5. Admin users cannot read private visit notes.
6. No double booking: pending appointments reserve their slot, doctors cannot overlap, and patients cannot overlap across doctors.
7. Preserve portfolio-level UI quality.
8. Responsive behavior is required.
9. Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
10. A phase is not complete until its verification succeeds.
